Red Flags — Products to Avoid

Excluded despite appearing in trend lists — here's why

📡

GPS Pet Tracker

Hardware market saturated. Margins only 25–35%. Requires SIM/subscription for real value. One-time hardware sale = no recurring revenue.

🧤

Silicone Grooming Glove

Amazon UK already priced at £5.94 with 900+ monthly sales. No margin headroom after shipping and ads. Race to the bottom.

🦮

Standard Dog Lead / Harness

Dominated by established brands: Halti, Julius-K9, Ruffwear. Near-impossible to compete on trust and brand recognition.

⚾

Automatic Ball Launcher

Heavy and fragile = high shipping cost and high return rate. Margin is entirely destroyed by one return or one breakage claim.

🍖

Pet Food / Treats

Regulated product category in UK. Requires legal review, specific labelling laws, and trading standards compliance. Not suitable for generic dropshipping.
